# Dereth Online — How to Play

> A multiplayer top-down action RPG. Asheron's Call meets Vampire Survivors.
> Sign in with Google. No download required — runs in the browser.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Sign In
Click **Sign in with Google** on the login screen. Your progress is saved to your account automatically.

### 2. Create a Character
Choose your **race** — each has a distinct look on the map:
- **Aluvian** — golden
- **Gharundim** — blue-purple
- **Sho** — green
- **Viamontian** — red
- **Umbraen** — dark purple

Then **distribute your starting attribute points** across 6 attributes:

| Attribute | Affects |
|-----------|---------|
| **STR** (Strength) | Melee damage |
| **COORD** (Coordination) | Attack accuracy |
| **QUICK** (Quickness) | Move speed |
| **END** (Endurance) | Max HP |
| **FOCUS** | Magic effectiveness |
| **SELF** | Skill progression |

You can't change these later at creation — spend them wisely.

### 3. The Hub (Lifestone)
After creating your character you land in **the Hub** — your home base. From here you can:
- View and manage your **gear and backpack**
- Spend **XP on attributes and skills**
- Enter **the Dungeon** to fight enemies and earn loot
- **Deploy** into the open world

---

## The World

Once deployed you're in the open world. Other players appear as colored circles with their name above them. You appear as a white dot on the minimap.

### Movement
| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `WASD` or `↑↓←→` | Move |
| `F` | Enter a portal (when standing near one) |
| `P` | Cast a recall portal back to the Hub |
| `C` | Open / close the character panel |
| `R` | Respawn after death |

---

## Combat

Combat is **automatic** — your character attacks the nearest enemy within range every half second. You don't click to attack; just walk into the fight.

Your weapon determines your attack style:
- **⚔️ War Sword / 🪓 War Axe** — melee swing arc
- **🔮 Sceptre** — traveling magic orb that flies to the target
- **No weapon** — unarmed punch

Enemies drop **loot** when killed. Walk over it to pick it up automatically — the game always equips the best item for each slot.

### Enemy Types
| Enemy | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Drudge `D` | Common, weak |
| Olthoi `O` | Medium threat, good XP |
| Shadow `S` | Fast, medium size |
| Tusker `T` | Large, high XP |
| Virindi `V` | Magic-using, dangerous |
| Banderling `B` | Tough, beefy |
| Larva `l` | Tiny, swarms |

**Bosses** are 2× larger, glow with an aura, and always drop armor when killed.

---

## The Dungeon

Enter the dungeon from the Hub to fight progressively harder floors. Each floor has a **boss** that spawns every 60 seconds.

- Dungeon enemies are tougher than the open world but drop better loot
- Killing the boss always drops an **armor piece** (head, chest, hands, or feet)
- Clear higher floors to earn better gear and more XP
- Use `F` near the dungeon exit portal to return to the Hub

## Gear & Equipment

There are **6 gear slots**:

| Slot | Icon on HUD |
|------|-------------|
| Weapon | W |
| Head | H |
| Chest | C |
| Hands | G |
| Feet | F |
| Trinket | T |

### Rarity Tiers
Items have 6 rarity tiers — higher rarity = better stats:

| Tier | Color |
|------|-------|
| Common | Gray |
| Uncommon | Green |
| Rare | Blue |
| Epic | Purple |
| Legendary | Orange |
| Ancient | Gold |

### Item Stats
| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| `dm` | Damage multiplier |
| `ar` | Armor (damage reduction %) |
| `hp` | Bonus max HP |
| `sp` | Move speed bonus |
| `as` | Attack speed bonus |
| `xp` | XP gain bonus |

Every item can roll a **bonus secondary stat** on top of its primary.

### The Character Panel (`C`)
Open the panel in-game to:
- See all equipped gear and your backpack
- **Click equipped items** to unequip them
- **Click backpack items** to equip them
- **Spend XP** on attributes and skills

---

## The Bottom HUD

The bar at the bottom of the screen shows:

- **Left — Red orb:** your current HP. The liquid fills based on how much HP you have.
  Below it: your armor rating (`AR %`) and level.
- **Center — 6 slots:** your equipped gear. Borders are colored by rarity.
- **Right — Minimap:** a 96px circular map showing:
  - **White dot** = you (always center)
  - **Red dots** = enemies in range
  - **Orange dots** = bosses in range
  - **Blue dots** = other players in range
  - **Edge ticks** = direction to closest enemy/player outside range

---

## XP & Progression

You earn XP by killing enemies. XP is spent in two ways:

**Attributes** — raise your 6 core stats (STR, COORD, etc.) to increase raw power.

**Skills** — invest in combat and utility skills:
- Heavy Weapons, Light Weapons, Missile Weapons
- War Magic, Life Magic, Item Magic
- Melee Defense, Run, Alchemy

Higher skills make you dramatically more effective in those areas. There are no class restrictions — build however you want.

---

## Portals

| Portal | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| Hub portal | Teleports you back to the Lifestone (home base) |
| Dungeon portal | Enters/exits a dungeon floor |
| `P` key | Starts a 3-second cast to create your own recall portal |

When you die you are automatically returned to the Lifestone.

---

## Tips

- **Walk toward enemies** — auto-attack handles the rest
- **Pick up everything** — the game auto-equips the best item, so looting is always worth it
- **Spend your XP** — unspent XP does nothing; open the character panel (`C`) and allocate it
- **Watch the minimap** — edge ticks warn you about enemies approaching from off-screen
- **Boss drops armor** — prioritize killing the dungeon boss every 60 seconds for guaranteed armor upgrades
- **Co-op scales well** — the more players in a world, the harder and more rewarding it gets
